CardWise

Privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026 · Published by Oren Horak · {{EMAIL}}

The short version. CardWise tells you which of your own credit cards is likely to earn the most where you are. Your wallet lives on your iPhone. CardWise has no servers that receive your cards, and never asks for a card number, security code, expiry date or bank login.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The cards you add, their rewards rules, nicknames, and any photo you take of a card. Photos are straightened on the device; CardWise does not read any text on the card.
  • Your settings, point values and notification preferences.
  • The list of places CardWise noticed you arriving at, and a record of which reminders it sent or held back (kept for up to four days).
  • Your Impact record: which reminders you said you used, and any amounts you chose to type in.

None of this is uploaded. Settings › Erase everything in the app deletes all of it, and so does deleting the app.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • Your location, to Google. To find shops near you — for the map, and to choose which shops to watch for arrival reminders — CardWise sends your position, and anything you type into the map's search, to the Google Places API. Nothing that identifies you is attached, though Google receives your device's internet (IP) address as with any web request. Shop photos shown in the app also come from Google. Google's handling is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Address suggestions, from Apple. What you type in the map's search box is also sent to Apple Maps for address suggestions, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Links you open. Opening a shop's website, directions or a card issuer's terms hands you to Safari, Phone or Maps, which apply their own policies.

Location permission

CardWise asks for Always location so that iOS can tell it when you arrive at a shop while the app is closed. iOS does this with geofences — circles around up to 20 nearby shops — and occasional updates when you have moved a few hundred metres, not continuous GPS. You can use the wallet and the map without Always, or with no location at all. Arrival reminders need Always and Precise Location. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings › CardWise › Location.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

CardWise shows no advertising, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not sell or share your information.

Children

CardWise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Recommendations are estimates

CardWise is for information only and is not financial, credit or tax advice. It is not affiliated with any bank, card issuer or card network. Your card issuer's terms decide what you actually earn.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change, and significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
