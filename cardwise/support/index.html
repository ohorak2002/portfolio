CardWise

Support

CardWise is an iPhone app that tells you which of your own credit cards to pay with, and can remind you when you walk into a shop where one of them earns more.

Get in touch: CardWise.help@outlook.com

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the version number shown at the bottom of the app's More tab.

Reminders are not arriving

  • Open the iPhone's Settings › CardWise › Location. Choose Always, and make sure Precise Location is on — iOS does not report arriving somewhere without it.
  • In the same place, check Notifications are allowed.
  • A reminder comes a few minutes after you arrive, and only where one of your cards earns more than the others. At shops where every card earns the same, CardWise stays quiet on purpose.
  • In the app, Settings › Recent activity shows where CardWise noticed you arriving and what it decided each time.

A card's rates look wrong

Card terms change. Open the card in your wallet to see where its details came from and when they were checked, correct them yourself, or email us and we will update them. Your card issuer's terms always decide what you actually earn.

Deleting your data

Everything CardWise knows about you is on your iPhone. More › Settings › Erase everything deletes it, and so does deleting the app. There is no account to close.

Good to know

CardWise never asks for a card number or bank login. It is for information only and is not financial advice, and it is not affiliated with any bank or card issuer.
